About Capitabel · Founded 2023 · Chennai · Manor Group

Twenty-five years of
South India ground.
Codified into a lender.

Capitabel Solutions was founded in 2023 by Chidambaram and Harish - two operators with deeply complementary expertise: 15+ years in South India real estate development, and 18+ years in banking technology built across Europe.

The founding insight: millions of creditworthy borrowers in peri-urban and Tier 2 South India are underserved - not because lenders won't lend to them, but because the matching infrastructure between borrower and lender does not exist at the ground level. Built on Manor Group's 25+ years of South India real estate operations, we're now codifying that ground-level knowledge into technology.
